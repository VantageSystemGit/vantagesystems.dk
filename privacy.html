Vantage Systems I/S (CVR 46453344), Denmark, is the data controller for personal data processed through vantagesystems.dk. Contact us at cw@vantagesystems.dk with any question about your data.

Who processes data for us

We use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf under data processing agreements:

  • GitHub (GitHub Pages): website hosting. GitHub is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.
  • Simply.com (Denmark): domain and DNS.
  • Formspree, Inc. (USA): receives demo requests sent through the form on this website and forwards them to our email. We have a data processing agreement with Formspree, and transfers outside the EU are covered by the safeguards in that agreement.
  • Microsoft (Microsoft 365): our email. Microsoft is certified under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with anyone else unless the law requires it.

About Foresight's data

Our product, Foresight, analyses public company information from official registers such as the Danish Business Authority. It does not use this website's visitor data.

Your rights

You have the right to access the data we hold about you, to have it corrected or deleted, to restrict or object to our processing, and to receive your data in a portable format. Email us and we will reply within one month.

You can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet), Carl Jacobsens Vej 35, 2500 Valby, datatilsynet.dk.

Changes
